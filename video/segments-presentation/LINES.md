## `01-this-account.mp4` — this account

A real account on Solana. The chain says it holds nothing.

It holds a hundred and seventy-three thousand shares of tokenized stock.

## `02-nobody-allowed.mp4` — and nobody has been allowed one

Every tokenized stock on Solana can do that. All 1,992 of them, from three issuers with nothing to do with each other. So I counted the accounts actually using it. Across nearly half a million: two have tried, and none has been allowed.

## `03-confide.mp4` — Confide

That is what Confide is for: confidential delivery-versus-payment for tokenized stocks. It builds the zero-knowledge proofs the chain will not assemble for you, and lets each side read the other's amount before signing. Nobody stands in the middle.

## `04-the-trade.mp4` — the trade

Two holders exchange a position for stablecoins in a single transaction. Either both sides settle or neither does. Nobody watching learns the size, the price it implies, or that either of them held anything.

## `05-why-shut.mp4` — why the door is shut

Token-2022 gives the issuer plenty: freeze a transfer, seize a holder's tokens, run their own code. Its confidential-transfer extension has exactly one auditor key, and that key reads everybody or nobody. No setting shows one balance to one regulator — so all 1,992 left it empty.

## `06-why-now.mp4` — why now

Last week the SEC gave tokenized stock five years of relief in the United States. Then you read the conditions: trading only through an automated market maker, where the size and direction of every trade you make is published within ten minutes. A desk cannot go there.

## `07-the-turn.mp4` — so the first trade is an issuance

So the first trade through that door is not a swap between two holders. It is an issuance — because the issuer is the one who can open the account.

## `08-the-gate.mp4` — the gate, both ways

An issuer allocates twenty thousand shares. Sent before the issuer signed for the account, the chain refuses it. One instruction later, the same transaction settles.

## `09-why-not-an-exchange.mp4` — why not just use an exchange

And you cannot do this on an exchange. The exemption covers trading executed by an automated market maker, where every fill's size goes on a public tape within ten minutes. That is the venue, not one pool.

## `10-what-i-got-wrong.mp4` — what I got wrong, and what you can run

One thing I got wrong: a review found the check I called the safety step was signing something it had never compared. It is fixed, and every review is committed. This is all devnet, and you can run it yourself: one command opens a confidential position on an issuer gated exactly as all 1,992 are.
