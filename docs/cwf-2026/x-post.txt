Every tokenized stock on Solana ships confidential balances.

All 1,992 of them.

I counted the accounts actually using one.

Zero.

━━━

So I built the thing they are for.

50,000 tokenized shares against $8,750,000, in one Solana transaction. Delivery against payment. Either both legs settle or neither does.

Every account involved still reports a public balance of 0. Nobody watching learns the size, or the price it implies, or that either party held anything.

━━━

A loan needs somebody in the middle. A trade needs nobody — the transaction is the clearing house.

━━━

That is not the design I set out to build. I spent a month on a loan against these positions and hit the same wall every time: collateral needs a third party to hold it, and a third party needs an account.

No issuer will approve one. autoApproveNewAccounts is false on 1,992 of 1,992 — a confidential account cannot exist until the issuer signs for it, and across 330,266 live accounts none has. Shipped, gated, unused.

Not an equities story either. PYUSD and USDG land on the identical configuration: gate shut, auditor slot empty. PayPal's dollar ships the same unusable privacy feature behind the same door.

Four issuers, two asset classes, one dead end. Nobody chose this — it is what the substrate does.

And no, you cannot just use a pool. Its reserves are public, and a trade moves them by exactly the amount traded — so anything settled against one publishes the size, whatever the token can do.

━━━

Kamino refuses a confidential deposit, and they are right to. A lender who cannot read a balance cannot price it. I quote their own source and concede it before saying anything about the gap.

Nobody outside my repository has used any of this. No pilot, no issuer asked. Traction is zero and I would rather say so than have you find it.
