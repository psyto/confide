## The questions

**Please attack the premises before the tactics.**

1. **Is the replacement bet actually scoring?** The claim is that a number a judge can recompute
   beats a number a stranger agreed to say. Does that answer *potential market size* and
   *viability* for a judge who reads four non-engineering criteria first — or does it answer a
   question nobody asked, very well?

2. **Traction.** Is there any honest reading under which something already true counts as traction,
   or is zero simply zero? And separately: is instrumenting the public page worth doing — does
   "eleven people outside the repo opened the decision page, here are the dates" move a judge, or
   does it read as a vanity metric and cost credibility? If it is worth doing, is 24 days enough for
   the number to be non-embarrassing?

3. **Founder + market fit is unaddressed anywhere in the repository.** It is one of the four read
   first. Is that a form-filling problem, or does it need evidence built now?

4. **What is the highest-value thing left that does not require a stranger?** Rank against these:
   the CWF submission text; the two submission videos; instrumenting use; extending the packet to
   every live mint (week 3 in the plan); the three weekly check-ins remaining; a control case that
   returns `PASS` rather than `REQUIRES INTEGRATION`.

5. **What in the current plan is theatre?** Name anything that consumes the remaining days and
   cannot change a judge's score. The plan already refuses more documentation-integrity work and
   more mechanism; I want to know what else should be cut.

6. **Is there a move nobody has considered?** Both previous strategy reviews worked inside the frame
   of "build an artifact, approach curators". If there is a different shape — a partner prize, a
   different track, a collaboration, a public artefact that travels on its own — say it plainly,
   including if it means retiring the second bet the way the first was retired.

**Be specific about what you could not verify.** On 2026-09-16 you estimated four line numbers from
URLs in an environment that could not clone, and all four were wrong. Say when you are inferring.
