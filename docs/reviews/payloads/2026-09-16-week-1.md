## The claims, in the order that matters

**A week-1 error propagates into three more weeks, so please attack the facts before the framing.**

1. **The four-line gap.** I claim `constraints.rs:187`, `:194`, `:201` and `:131` mean a Kamino
   reserve will not touch a token account that can hold value confidentially, and that
   `closable()` failing means any non-zero confidential balance is refused. Is that reading right?
2. **The paths.** I claim the check runs on the *user's own* account and not only the reserve
   vault, on deposit, borrow and both sides of liquidation
   (`lending_checks.rs:186`, `:102`, `:60`, `:255`, `:260`). **The claim I most want attacked: is
   there any klend instruction that moves value in or out of a reserve and does NOT run this
   check?** If one exists, "a confidential holder cannot deposit, borrow or be liquidated" is
   false and the whole week is built on it.
3. **1,869 of 1,869 admissible.** `klend_rules.evaluate_mint` is my transcription of
   `check_only_supported_extensions_on_liquidity_mint`. Does it match — allow-list membership and
   each conditional branch? Does it miss a condition, or invent one? Note it evaluates mint-level
   rules only, which I state but may not state clearly enough.
4. **The reserve layout.** `klend_rules.decode_reserve` reads LTV, liquidation threshold, caps and
   balances by byte offset. It was derived from the source and then checked against mainnet — the
   first derivation had `mint_decimals` eight bytes late because it assumed `u128` aligns to 16,
   which is true on x86 and false on BPF. **Are `OFF_CONFIG = 4856` and the field offsets under it
   correct, or did I land on numbers that happen to decode plausibly for these particular
   reserves?** `borrow_factor_pct = 150` and `name = "SPCX"` were my self-checks; that is thin.
5. **The oracle decode** in `scripts/packet.sh` (TokenInfo at `OFF_CONFIG + 176`): Scope feed,
   chain index 490, `max_age_price_seconds` 180, and a price band of $60–$200 from the heuristic
   with `exp = 2`. Is the heuristic actually a price band in dollars, or am I misreading its units?
6. **19 reserves.** I filter all 591 reserves by whether the liquidity mint is in `web/mints.json`.
   Is `getProgramAccounts` with that discriminator returning what I think, and is the deposited
   total (~89,195 tokens across 13) computed from the right field?

## The argument

If the facts hold, the week's claim is: **Kamino already lends against tokenized stocks at LTVs its
market owners chose, SpaceX among them, every position public; the only thing stopping a
confidential position is four lines; and Confide has two of the four things that would be needed to
change that, with the other two named and not built.**

7. Does that argument follow from the facts, or does it need something I have not established?
8. `docs/packets/SPCX.US.md` §7 lists what would make a refusal correct, including one the packet
   cannot answer (a permanent delegate can move collateral the lender cannot see). **Is §7 honest
   enough, or is it performing honesty while omitting a harder objection?**
9. The empty SPCX reserve (0.1 deposited). `KAMINO.md` says the likely reason is that Backpack's
   tokens are newer and thinner, not that privacy is the binding constraint, and that "an empty
   reserve proves suppressed demand" is untestable here. Is that the right call, or am I
   discarding a signal?
10. **Does week 1 leave weeks 2–4 standing?** The plan's remaining beats are: the decision page
    reaching a stranger, coverage across every mint, the videos, and outreach as upside. If
    something in week 1 is wrong or thin enough to undermine those, say which beat dies.
