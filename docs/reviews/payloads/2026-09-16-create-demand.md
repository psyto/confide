# Second pass — the founder rejected the conservative plan. Now give me the aggressive one.

You told me yesterday to spend 3–4 of 27 days on code and 12 on customer discovery, and to refuse
mainnet, price feeds, on-chain floor verification and partial seizure. It is a good answer to the
question "how do I score well on seven judging criteria with what I have".

**The founder has chosen a different question, deliberately and with eyes open:**

> Tokenized equities have barely started. Think from where that goes, not from what Confide is
> today. I want to **create demand rather than discover it**, and to get materially further ahead
> than anyone else — even if that carries large risk.

That is a real decision by the person whose project it is. Do not talk them out of it and do not
re-litigate the conservative plan. Give them the best version of the path they chose.

## What you should weigh

- **The binding constraint is the issuer.** Every one of the 1,869 mints has confidential transfers
  on, the auditor key null, and `autoApproveNewAccounts: false`. A holder cannot open a confidential
  account on `NVDAx` without Backed signing. So the interesting question is not "who buys this" but
  "what makes the issuer's signature the obviously correct move, or makes it unnecessary".
- **The slot is empty because no setting of it is correct.** Token-2022 offers one global auditor
  key. Confide is a disclosure model that makes a setting correct. If that is right, Confide is not
  a product looking for a buyer — it is a **missing standard**, and standards create demand by being
  adopted rather than sold.
- **What already runs** is in the README's evidence list: the embargo (I1–I5), the corporate-action
  restatement that refuses rather than guessing, confidential collateral proved over an account's
  own ciphertext, and seizure end to end on devnet.
- **27 days, one person, four weekly one-minute check-ins** that judges watch as a sequence.

## What I want

1. **The single most demand-creating move available in 27 days.** Not a list — your pick, and the
   argument for why it beats the runners-up. Consider at least: publishing this as a standard or
   profile others implement; doing the thing on the live mints to the exact limit the issuer's gate
   allows and making the missing signature publicly visible; attacking a bigger adjacent layer the
   whole space will need; and anything I have not thought of.
2. **What "materially further ahead than anyone else" would concretely mean here** — what does the
   rest of the field not have, and what would make that gap legible to a judge in a 3-minute video?
3. **The risks, priced.** The founder accepts large risk; that is not licence to be vague about it.
   What could go badly, how badly, and which risks are survivable versus project-ending.
4. **What this path forces us to give up.** Be explicit: if we do this, what from the conservative
   plan dies, and what is the cost of it dying.
5. **A 27-day shape** with the four check-in dates as beats, and a stated point of no return —
   the date after which the aggressive path can no longer be abandoned safely.

Assume competence and speed on the engineering. Assume no budget, no company, no network in this
market, and no permission from anyone. Do not praise. Do not summarise the project back to me.
