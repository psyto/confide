Week 1 check-in for Colosseum's Crypto World's Fair. Fifty-five seconds, three questions.

WHAT CHANGED

Confide was a loan against tokenized stock. This week it became delivery versus payment. 50,000 shares against $8,750,000, one Solana transaction, neither side publishing what moved. Every account involved still reports a public balance of 0.

WHAT I LEARNED

The loan kept hitting one wall: collateral needs a third party to hold it, and that account exists only if an issuer signs for it. None has — and I have not asked one, so what is measured is the door nobody has been through, not a refusal. So I counted how many ever have. Across more than 300,000 live token accounts on Apple, NVIDIA, SpaceX, Anthropic and AMC — two have configured one, both on NVDAx, and zero are approved. Somebody is knocking; the door has not opened. (The video says zero configured: that was true when it was cut on 21 September and the second account appeared that night.) A trade needs no third party at all, because a Solana transaction cannot half-happen.

WHAT IS NEXT

→ The swap, runnable by a stranger and not just by me
→ The gate on a real mint — a conversation nobody has had
✕ Matching. Bringing buyers and sellers together is what makes an exchange, and this is deliberately not one.

Traction is zero. No pilot, no user, no issuer asked, and I would rather say it than have you find it.

https://psyto.github.io/confide/
